# Awesome-Telemedicine

## Top Telemedicine Platforms Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on Virtual Visits, Secure Video Consultations, Patient Engagement, Scheduling, EHR Integration & Remote Care Delivery*

**Last updated: August 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Telemedicine / Telehealth**. These tools enable secure video and audio consultations, virtual waiting rooms, patient intake, e-prescribing support, and remote care delivery for clinics, health systems, and independent providers.



**Examples** include Doxy.me, Amwell, Teladoc Health, Zoom for Healthcare, Mend, Updox, Coviu, SimplePractice, Tebra, and Klara (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: There is a healthy ecosystem of open-source telehealth solutions. This section is expanded with mature projects such as OpenEMR (with telehealth modules), HCW@Home, full-stack telemedicine applications, and WebRTC-based platforms that organizations can self-host for greater control and lower cost.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[OpenEMR](https://github.com/openemr/openemr)**  

  The most popular open-source electronic health records and practice management system; supports telehealth integrations and has community modules for virtual care.



- **[HCW@Home (Health Care Worker @Home)](https://github.com/hcw-home)**  

  Fully open-source, institution-level secure teleconsultation system with chat, audio, and video (WebRTC), patient invites, and OpenEMR connectivity.



- **[AIOTP – All In One Telehealth Platform](https://github.com/ciips-code/aiotp)**  

  Open-source telehealth suite (commissioned with PAHO/WHO involvement) combining OpenEMR-based clinical records with Jitsi-based videoconferencing.



- **[HealthBridge and similar full-stack telehealth apps](https://github.com/)**  

  Modern full-stack open-source platforms offering appointment booking, video consultations, chat, and patient/doctor dashboards.



- **[MediConnect and comparable projects](https://github.com/)**  

  Next.js / modern-stack open telemedicine applications with real-time video, waiting rooms, digital prescriptions, and role-based dashboards.



- **[Open-source EMR + Telemedicine modules](https://github.com/opensource-emr/Telemedicine)**  

  Dedicated open telemedicine projects that connect doctors and patients via video, chat, and basic clinical documentation.



- **[Jitsi Meet and healthcare-adapted forks](https://github.com/jitsi)**  

  Popular open-source WebRTC video platform frequently customized and self-hosted for telehealth use cases.



- **[SMS-to-phone and low-bandwidth telemedicine tools](https://github.com/)**  

  Open projects designed for regions with limited internet, connecting patients via SMS to phone consultations.



- **[AI-assisted telehealth prototypes](https://github.com/)**  

  Community projects adding LLM-powered chat, transcription, or triage features on top of video consultation platforms.



- **[Self-hosted video + scheduling stacks](https://github.com/)**  

  Combinations of open scheduling systems, WebRTC servers, and simple EHR modules assembled into lightweight telehealth solutions.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- BigBlueButton and other open web-conferencing platforms adapted for clinical use.

- FHIR-enabled open EHR systems that can be extended with video visit workflows.

- Open source patient portal and messaging components.

- Docker-compose ready telehealth stacks for rapid local or private-cloud deployment.

- Community documentation and best-practice guides for HIPAA/GDPR-aligned self-hosting.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Start with **OpenEMR** or **HCW@Home** for the clinical and consultation core, add **Jitsi** (or LiveKit/Mediasoup) for video, implement secure authentication and audit logging, and integrate scheduling and basic billing. For lighter needs, deploy a modern full-stack open telehealth application and harden it for privacy and security requirements. Self-hosted solutions give full data ownership and can significantly reduce per-provider costs, but require proper infrastructure, security hardening, BAAs where applicable, and clinical workflow validation.

## Disclaimer

- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

- Telemedicine platforms handle protected health information and must comply with applicable regulations (HIPAA, GDPR, local telehealth laws, licensing, prescribing rules). Open-source solutions provide excellent transparency and cost control but require careful security configuration, legal review, and operational processes before clinical use.

- Always verify that any platform meets the regulatory, liability, and reimbursement requirements of your jurisdiction and specialty.
